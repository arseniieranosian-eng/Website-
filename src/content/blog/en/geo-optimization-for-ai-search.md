More and more people ask AI assistants instead of running a classic search. ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews answer directly – and cite only a handful of sources. **GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)** is the set of practices that helps you become one of them.

## Why GEO does not replace SEO

AI tools draw on websites that are well indexed, trustworthy and easy to understand. Good SEO is the foundation – GEO builds on top of it.

## 5 steps you can take today

1. **Answer directly.** Every important page should answer its core question clearly in the first paragraph.
2. **Structured data.** Mark up your business, people, services and FAQs with schema.org so machines understand who you are.
3. **A consistent entity.** The same name, address and description on your site, Google Business Profile, directories and LinkedIn.
4. **Mentions beyond your site.** AI often cites industry articles, directories and reviews. Build them just like links.
5. **Measure.** Regularly ask AI assistants your key questions and track who they recommend.
